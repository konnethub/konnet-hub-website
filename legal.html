Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Summary: Konnet Hub collects only what it needs to connect customers with local businesses. We do not sell your data. Ever. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how you can control it — in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR).

1. Who We Are

Konnet Hub is Nigeria's AI-powered local business finder, operated by Konnet Hub ("we", "us", "our"). We are accessible via WhatsApp (wa.me/2348104175328), Telegram (t.me/Konnet_Bot), and our website at konnethub.com.

For privacy enquiries, contact us at hello@konnethub.com.

2. Data We Collect

From Customers (people searching for businesses)

  • WhatsApp or Telegram user ID (automatically provided by the platform)
  • Search queries you send (e.g. "plumber in Yaba")
  • Location references mentioned in your searches

From Business Owners (registered listings)

  • Business name, category, and description
  • Location (LGA and state)
  • Contact information (phone number, WhatsApp number)
  • Payment information (processed securely via Paystack — we do not store card details)

From Website Visitors

  • Pages visited and time spent (via Google Analytics)
  • Device type, browser, and approximate location
  • Referring website or link

3. Why We Collect This Data

  • To match customer searches with relevant local businesses
  • To maintain and display business listings
  • To process subscription payments
  • To improve platform performance and search accuracy
  • To send service-related updates to registered businesses

4. Legal Basis (NDPR Compliance)

We process your data on the following lawful bases under the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) 2019:

  • Consent — when you register your business or use our platform
  • Contract — to deliver the services you have paid for
  • Legitimate interest — to improve platform performance and prevent abuse

5. How We Share Data

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with:

  • Paystack — to process payments (governed by Paystack's privacy policy)
  • Google Analytics — anonymised website usage data
  • WhatsApp / Telegram — messages are processed through their respective platforms
  • Law enforcement — only when legally required

6. Data Retention

  • Free listings: deleted 7 days after expiry if not renewed
  • Paid listings: retained for the duration of the subscription + 30 days after cancellation
  • Search logs: retained for up to 12 months for analytics purposes
  • Payment records: retained for 7 years as required by Nigerian financial regulations

7. Your Rights

Under the NDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Bureau (NDPB)

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@konnethub.com.

8. Children's Privacy

Konnet Hub is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify registered businesses of significant changes via WhatsApp or email. Continued use of the platform after changes constitutes acceptance.
